2 Vectorization settings

Inspects the image and picks a preset for you.

Removal is a flood fill from the image border, so artwork that merely shares the background colour is not touched.

Turns letter counters (the holes in B, O, e) into true transparent cutouts. Leave off if your artwork has genuine dark areas matching the background.

Advanced

Working resolution of the artwork. Higher keeps more detail but produces more paths and takes longer.

Cleans sub-pixel noise off colour-region boundaries before tracing, so curves come out as curves instead of many short straight segments. This is the main fix for jagged outlines on compressed artwork. Turn it down if fine detail is being rounded away.

Traces at a higher resolution and scales back down via the viewBox. This is what keeps curves and letterform corners smooth; turning it off is faster but visibly blockier.
